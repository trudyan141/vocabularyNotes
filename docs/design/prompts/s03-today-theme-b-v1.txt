Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: one high-fidelity mobile PWA screen, S03 Today for VocabularyNotes. This is a new visual composition, generated from scratch, not an edit of the previous concept.

Primary request: Design an exceptionally tasteful, warm and playful English vocabulary learning app that adults enjoy and children understand. This must feel like a carefully art-directed consumer learning product with a distinctive paper-and-word-cards identity. The rejected previous design was a generic blue dashboard with a huge numeral and a stock book illustration. Reconsider hierarchy, composition, illustration, surface treatment and typography, not merely a color swap. The visual references studied on Inspo were Headspace's friendly editorial illustration and Daybridge's airy, purposeful pastel groupings. Borrow principles, never their brand or mascot. Do not copy a marketing website into the app.

Output: EXACTLY ONE edge-to-edge portrait UI screenshot, logical viewport about 390 x 844, at high image resolution. No phone frame, no surrounding background, no multiple screens, no moodboard or annotations. All sections and the three-item bottom navigation must be visible. Vietnamese text must be clean, correct and easy to read, with every accent rendered accurately.

Art direction: “Paper playground” — a personal collection of words made from paper notes, cheerful but composed. Warm near-white background #FFFCF6. Aubergine ink #34243C. Primary button dark plum #4A2D5C, white text. One butter-yellow review surface #F8DF80. Soft lilac #E5DAF2 for the secondary capture action; mint #DCECDC and pale peach #F7E0D4 for lesson covers. Small coral and leaf-green details in illustrations. NO blue anywhere. NO corporate/royal-blue buttons. NO giant numeral used as a hero. NO gradients, glassmorphism, blurry blobs, stock 3D icons, clouds of sparkles or identical SaaS cards. No leaderboard, streak badge or progress ring. Make surfaces feel like curated stationery through a slim paper fold or edge, not texture noise or thick cartoon outlines. Typography is a friendly precise sans with Vietnamese coverage, similar to Be Vietnam Pro with rounded forms; distinctive weights and calm spacing, no serif or handwriting body text. Body about 16px, title about 28px, controls 48px high minimum. Use discipline: one memorable illustrated review panel, restrained UI everywhere else.

Composition from top to bottom, with 20px side margins:
1. Compact header: a tiny original two-paper-leaves mark in plum/lilac, the wordmark “VocabularyNotes” at the left, a neat circular account button at the right. The account button is quiet, not a large generic grey avatar. Under header, title “Hôm nay” and a small supporting line “Cùng ôn lại từ đã học”.
2. The signature review area, roughly 175–190px high: butter-yellow paper-like panel with asymmetric composition and generous breathing room. Left: “8 từ cần ôn” as a medium-sized natural heading, then “Một chút mỗi ngày”. Right: an original flat editorial illustration of two slightly overlapping paper vocabulary cards, one with a crisp red apple and word “apple”, the other with a small leaf/book motif. Precise hand-cut silhouettes, friendly and crafted, not a generic 3D textbook. Keep illustration small enough that learning action leads. Lower part: one generous full-width dark plum button “Ôn 5 phút”, small flashcard icon optional. The numeral 8 is the same size as the words, not gigantic.
3. Secondary capture row, separate from the review panel: low-height lilac tinted strip, simple outlined camera at left, title “Thêm ảnh bài học”, supporting text “Chụp hoặc chọn ảnh”, and a restrained disclosure chevron at right. It should feel obviously tappable yet visually secondary to the dark plum review button. No second filled CTA.
4. Calm search field with magnifier, text “Tìm từ đã học...”, fine neutral outline and modest 12px radius.
5. “Bài vừa học”: two lesson covers SIDE BY SIDE, about 140px high, like small personal notebooks rather than two generic full-width dashboard rows. First mint, with a simple pencil/notebook illustration, title “At school”, separate small lines “12 từ” and “2 mẫu câu”. Second pale peach, a simple bowl/fruit illustration, title “Food & drinks” wrapped naturally if needed, separate small lines “8 từ” and “1 mẫu câu”. Rounded 16px corners, subtle spine edge on each cover. No tiny labels, unnecessary arrows or busy decorations. The two covers remain anchored to the grid, not floating in space.
6. “Từ gần đây”: a compact typography-led row on the page, not another oversized card. Small red apple illustration left, “apple” in bold and “quả táo” beneath. Right: a plum outlined speaker button labelled “Nghe”. A small neutral note under it reads “Đã lưu trên máy”.
7. Bottom navigation on a clean warm-white surface: EXACTLY THREE equally spaced destinations “Hôm nay”, “Từ của tôi”, “Luyện tập”. Simple consistent line icons: home, open book, two flashcards. Hôm nay active with plum icon/text and a compact lilac capsule behind the icon; other destinations neutral ink. Labels readable and never truncated. Do not use a bar chart for Luyện tập.

Design judgment: visual hierarchy comes from editorial arrangement and selective color, not giant type or enclosing every element in a card. The review button is the single dominant action; camera, search, lesson covers and listen support it. This is the guest library with two EXISTING lessons and existing word apple; no cloud checkmark. All content matches the established S03 flow. Show the real useful interface, not a poster or onboarding marketing illustration. Cohesive spacing, optical alignment, polished Vietnamese, credible app proportions and accessible dark text on the pastel surfaces. Keep all required UI visible without cramming. No unrequested English slogans or extra menu items.
